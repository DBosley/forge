## The runner (`~/src/forge-goldfish`, 18 commits tonight)

Policy:
- attack styles (`attack: forge|focus_lowest|threat|spread`);
- charm modes, activation holds, optional trigger targets;
- Norin the Wary for opponents.

Accuracy:
- per-round metrics in every row;
- the round snapshot only runs with `-Dgoldfish.rounds=true`;
- `compare_runs.py` (IDENTICAL / EQUIVALENT / CHANGED / INCONCLUSIVE against a baseline, contract in `compare_runs.md`).

Speed:
- JIT flag, ParallelGC and the six fork flags applied by run.sh (`FORGE_FLAGS=` to run without them);
- `--fast on|off` (on by default with passive opponents);
- `--threads N` (heap sized automatically);
- `cpu_ms` per row;
- a 3600 s AI timeout.

Plumbing:
- `FORGE_SRC` can point at any worktree; build and work dirs are kept per worktree, and it rebuilds itself;
- a profile-dir race fix;
- `prof.py` for JFR queries, `simab.py` for timing ordinary `sim` games between two worktrees (how PRs 6-8 were measured).

Fast batch:

```
cd ~/src/forge-goldfish/prototype
./run.sh lazyperf -- --deck $PWD/decks/multiverse-reforged.dck --fish $PWD/decks/goldfish-basics.dck --games 1000 --seed 1 \
  --horizon 10 --opponents passive --opponent-stops upkeep,end_of_turn \
  --policy $PWD/policies/multiverse-reforged.yaml --threads 12 --out out/run.jsonl
```

Throughput on this machine, games per second (measured before the morning round; 320-game batches unless noted). With the morning commits, 1 × 12 does 1000 games in 61 s:

| Setup | Games/s |
|---|---|
| 16 processes × 1 thread | 4.4 |
| 4 × 4 | 7.9 |
| 2 × 8 | 9.2 |
| 1 × 12 | 11.2 (for 1000 games) |
| 1 × 16 | 11.2 (for 1000 games) |
| 1 × 32 | 6.9 |

The ceiling is the CPU. Past 12 threads every game just gets slower: 855 ms of CPU per game at 12 threads, 1104 ms at 16.

   - New baseline: `prototype/out/final-1000/` (rows and logs).

3. **Three of others' open PRs are in the fork.** If one of them changes in review, the fork has to follow it.
   - Our scan cuts sit in the fork as well, so PRs 6-8 follow the same rule.
4. **The static rebuild skip and the sequential attacks flag are gone from the fork** (reasons above).
5. **The forced-attackers PR draft makes no speed claim.** The order only matters when two or more creatures are forced to attack. Our deck's games came out identical with and without it.
